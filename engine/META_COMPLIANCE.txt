A. CONTENT (Community Standards)
 1. Violence & graphic content: no blood, wounds, dead bodies, executions, torture, accident/crash footage or victims.
    Pirates: battles shown only as ships, maps, cannons smoke from far away - never people being hurt; no "walking the plank" scenes with a person.
    Disasters (Port Royal, Pompeii, earthquakes): no bodies, no suffering people, no sensational death counts in the hook.
 2. Hateful conduct: never mock, insult or stereotype any religion, prophet, ethnicity, nationality or group. Sacred places
    (Kâbe, Mescid-i Aksa, Vatikan, Ayasofya, Uluru, Kailash, temples, churches, synagogues) are shown with respect;
    no jokes about faith; AyetBilim never attacks other faiths.
 3. Religious imagery: no depictions of prophets, sacred figures or faces (Islamic sensitivity + safest for all faiths).
 4. Dangerous organisations & individuals: no praise or symbols of designated groups. Pirates are presented as HISTORY
    (who, where, when, what archaeology found), never as heroes to copy. No modern piracy/terror groups at all.
 5. Misinformation: no false health claims, no "energy cures", no conspiracy theories (hidden Antarctica bases, aliens
    built the pyramids, fake disasters). Legends (ley lines, Mu, Atlantis, Bermuda, Piri Reis-Antarctica) are always
    framed "iddia vs kanıt" with the evidence stated plainly. Every number checked with 2 reliable sources.
 6. Politics: no current conflicts, elections, borders or ownership disputes (Jerusalem, Tibet, Ayasofya's status,
    Cyprus...). History and architecture only.
 7. Adult content: none (no nudity, including in paintings/statues - choose another subject or crop).
 8. Regulated goods & dangerous acts: no weapons how-to, no "build your own plane and fly it" (Kokpit: education only),
    no drugs/alcohol focus, no dangerous challenges.
 9. Children: never show identifiable children; no content aimed at sexualising or targeting minors (obviously).
