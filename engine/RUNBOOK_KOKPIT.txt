## 3. Build the Blender scene: `scene.py` in the project folder
```python
import sys, os; sys.path.insert(0, os.path.expanduser('~/eng/kokpit_blender')); import kb
P = os.path.dirname(os.path.abspath(__file__)); T = kb.Timeline(P); kb.setup(T.duration)
A = kb.light_aircraft(stage=<stage>)          # everything built in earlier episodes + this one
# THIS episode's part arrives / moves on its words:
kb.fly_in(A['ribs'], T.cut(3), T.cut(3) + 1.4, (0, 0, 2.5), stagger=0.03)
kb.glow(A['spar'], T.word('kiriş', 2))
# one camera per line, always moving, cut at T.cut(i):
kb.shot(T.cut(0), (7, -9, 4.5), (9, -7, 4), (0, -0.5, 1.3), lens=30)
p0, p1 = kb.orbit_points((0, -0.5, 1.3), 10, 3, -30, 20); kb.shot(T.cut(1), p0, p1, (0, -0.5, 1.3), lens=32)
if '--hero' in sys.argv:                       # thumbnail subject (transparent PNG)
    kb.hero_render(A['spar'] + A['ribs'], os.path.join(P, 'review/hero.png'), (6, -6, 4), (2.5, 0, 1.5)); sys.exit()
kb.render_cli(P)
```
Kit (`kb.py`): parts `light_aircraft(stage)` (1 profil · 2 spar+ribs · 3 wing skin · 4 frames+stringers · 5 fuselage skin · 6 tail · 7 engine+propeller · 8 landing gear · 9 canopy), `airfoil_slab`, `wing`, `ribs`, `spar`, `fuselage_frames`, `stringers`, `fuselage_skin`, `tail`, `engine_block`, `propeller`, `landing_gear`, `canopy`, `box`, `curve_obj`, `arrow`; animation `fly_in`, `scale_in`, `show/hide`, `fade` (alpha), `glow` (emission pulse on the part being explained), `spin`, `hinge`, `flow_lines` (glowing airflow dashes along polylines); cameras `shot`, `orbit_points` (depth of field on by default). Units = metres, +Y = nose, +Z = up.
Rules: the part of the episode is visible in second 1; camera always moves; a new camera every line (1.5-3 s); the explained piece glows on its word; airflow/forces shown as `flow_lines` / `arrow`; studio stays dark navy with the teal blueprint grid (distinctive asset). If the episode needs a part the kit does not have (aileron, flap, fuel tank, pitot tube, instruments…), ADD a function to kb.py in the same style and commit kb.py to `engine/kokpit_blender/` with the video.

## 3b. CINEMATIC LOOK — EVERY EPISODE (the user's rule since 2026-11-26)
The user gave two style references: (1) a cinematic animated seaplane-chase sequence from a feature film — canary-yellow plane, turquoise sea, blue sky, puffy clouds, sweeping chase cams, low hero angles, wide lenses, prop blur, turbulence shake, a storm act, fast 2-4 s cuts; (2) the "Mechanic Vision 3D" aircraft-systems Shorts (fuel system 297K views, landing gear, jet engine) — realistic 3D, skin turns see-through to show the system inside, colour-coded glowing flows, small label pills. Borrow the STYLE only: our own aircraft, no film characters, names, logos, shots or music.
Every video mixes TWO WORLDS, switched per line with `kb.look()`:
- **SKY** (line 0 hook, line 1 series line, last 1-2 lines payoff): the FINISHED yellow plane flying over the sea — "this is what we are building". Low hero chase (lens 20-26, shake 0.05-0.08), wide banking shot (lens 32-40, `fly_motion` bank 12-20°), from-behind chase (shake 0.1). In the payoff the episode's part WORKS in flight (aileron deflects in the bank, gear retracts, flow lines over the wing...).
- **STUDIO** (middle lines): the dark blueprint studio where this episode's part is built (stage plane). If the part is inside something, `kb.xray(skin, t0, t1)` turns the skin see-through (include the canopy). Flows colour-coded: fuel `glow_yellow`, air `glow_blue`/`glow_teal`, heat/exhaust `glow_red`, forces `arrow` orange. Max 2 `kb.label()` pills per shot, ≤3 Turkish words each, never in the caption zone.
- Presets: `day` (default), `sunset` (finale / milestone episodes), `storm` + `kb.lightning([t,...])` (weather/safety topics only). One preset per video.

**LESSONS FROM MECHANIC VISION 3D (5 aircraft Shorts watched with vidIQ, 2026-11-26) — apply every episode:**
Hits: fuel system 297K views, fuel system remake 12.8K, landing gear 8K. Flops: "what is inside an airplane" 1.1K, "how a jet engine works" 813.
1. TOPIC: a specific HIDDEN system people never see (fuel, landing gear, hydraulics, brakes, control cables) beats generic overviews ("what's inside a plane") and saturated topics (suck-squeeze-bang-blow jet engine). They remade the fuel system 4×; a winning topic can return as a deeper episode later.
2. FIRST FRAME: the plane in the sky ALREADY cut open (`kb.xray(FP[...], 0, 0.05)`) with the system glowing inside, and the narration's hook starts at 0.0 s. Never open on a silent or plain shot (the jet-engine flop had 2.5 s of silence). Avoid the cliché "Hiç merak ettin mi…" opener; open with the oddity/number (PLAYBOOK).
3. JOURNEY: follow ONE thing from start to end (a fuel drop: tank → pump → filter → nozzle → flame; a force: stick → cable → aileron). One 3-6 s `kb.path_shot()` fly-through along the same path as the `flow_lines` — the shot viewers remember.
4. PACE ≈ 3 cuts per 10 s (2 s shots), plus the one longer fly-through. A sound cue on every beat (whoosh, hiss, clunk, roar).
5. COLOURS: fuel amber/yellow, air blue, heat red/orange, hydraulics/electric teal; max 2 label pills per shot.
6. END: back in the sky, the system glowing while the plane flies, one summary line + next-part question over the moving picture. Never a static/black end card, no real-life stock footage (style break, rights).

Render cost: sky frames ≈ 9-10 s each on 2 CPUs (motion blur + sea), studio ≈ 5-7 s. Keep sky ≤ 40 % of the video; render in chunks of **1.7 s** (≈ 40 frames, < 10 min each). A 30 s episode ≈ 75-95 min. Reference test: `kokpit360_yeni_gorunum_test.mp4` (2026-11-26).

## 4. QA BEFORE the long render (ONE image)
Render 8 stills at the middle of 8 lines: `for t in <8 times>; do python3 scene.py --still $t review/s_$t.png; done` (~6 s each) and tile them into ONE 4x2 image (`ffmpeg -pattern_type glob -i 'review/s_*.png' -vf scale=270:480,tile=4x2 review/stills.jpg`). Look at that one image: subject in frame, nothing intersecting wrongly, not too dark/bright, nothing in the caption zone that matters (y 1330-1630 of 1920 = lower 30 %). Fix and re-check only changed shots.

## 5. Render (Cycles CPU, 540x960, ~5-10 s per frame -> 30 s video ≈ 75-95 min)
Render in foreground chunks of 1.7 s (sky frames are slower) so each command ends inside the 10-min limit (timeout 600000), e.g. `python3 scene.py --render 0 1.7`, `--render 1.7 3.4`, … until the end (`T.duration`). Frames go to `frames/f_####.png`; existing frames are skipped, so a chunk that was cut off is simply run again. Never run two chunks at the same time (2 CPUs). Do not raise samples or resolution: compose.py upscales to 1080x1920 with lanczos + sharpening.
